Why I made this

I live here.

I'm from Sterling. If you grew up around here you know the buildings. Long, gray, no windows, a fence, and a hum you stop noticing until you're somewhere quiet. I used to work security at one, so I've been on both sides of the fence.

Loudoun has more data centers than anywhere else on earth. Most of the conversation about them is about money and power lines. I wanted to map the part people actually live with: what the land used to be, and how hot it gets around them now, and how loud it is outside someone's house at night.

So this map does threetwo things. The land layer shows real satellite imagery year by year, so you can drag the slider and watch fields turn into roofs. The heat layer uses Landsat surface temperature from the summers before and after the boom. The noise layer is readings I took myself, walking around with a sound meter. Next up is noise. I'm going to walk around with a sound meter and map how loud it actually gets near these buildings.

None of this is a model guessing. Every layer is real data, and where I don't have data yet, the map just says so.

Sam Melaku GIS, UConn
